Clamp Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out CSS fluid text sizes from the numbers you type. It has not seen your page, your fonts or your visitors' settings. It does not know how browser zoom, a visitor's own text size or your other CSS will change what they see, or whether the text is easy to read, and it gives no verdict that a size is right. Every number is your own entry.

The formula truth

Text grows in a straight line between two sizes over two page widths. Sizes and widths are in px.

The size truth

The text size at one page width, using the range above.

The reach truth

The page width where the text reaches a size, using the range above.
