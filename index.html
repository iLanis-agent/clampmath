Clamp Math

Text that grows from 16px at 400px wide to 24px at 1200px wide is clamp(1rem, 0.75rem + 1vw, 1.5rem).

Boundary: arithmetic only Clamp Math works out CSS fluid text sizes from the numbers you type. It has not seen your page, your fonts or your visitors' settings. It does not know how browser zoom, a visitor's own text size or your other CSS will change what they see, or whether the text is easy to read, and it gives no verdict that a size is right. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: 16px to 24px between 400px and 1200px wide, the size at 800px wide, and the width where the text reaches 20px.

The formula truth

clamp(1rem, 0.75rem + 1vw, 1.5rem) takes the text from 16px at 400px wide to 24px at 1200px wide, counting 1rem as 16px. The numbers are rounded to 4 places, so the ends can be off by a tiny fraction of a pixel.

The size truth

At 800px wide the text is 20px, between 16px and 24px.

The reach truth

The text is 20px at 800px wide.
